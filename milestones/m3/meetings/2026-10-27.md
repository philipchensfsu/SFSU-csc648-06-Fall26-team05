We talked about using Gemini for PRD generation and follow-up questions. We discussed API
limits, budgets, and ways to keep the AI focused on PRD-related requests. We also brought up
prompt injection, but the exact limits and protections still need to be checked. The goal was to
finish the frontend by Wednesday so we could continue connecting it to the backend.

admin. Someone working alone can create an organization with just themselves, so we do not
need a separate flow for individual users. We also discussed letting admins create team members’
accounts directly instead of sending email invitations. Team members will access the same PRDs
and comments, with their permissions controlling what they can do. The backend team also
explained that comments can notify the PRD owner and assigned members.

management, and basic notifications. More detailed permissions, cross-department feedback, and

workflows and progress tracking, but leaned toward leaving them out of the immediate demo.

make their own commits, review PRs, and understand their code well enough to explain it during
